Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who runs Schritt

Schritt is operated by Schritt. Questions or requests about your data: pandyautsav@gmail.com.

What Schritt stores

Your lessons, progress, streak, gems, settings, chosen avatar and, if you create one, a profile name and email. Your lessons and progress stay in this browser's local storage on this device and we do not receive them. When you create a profile, or apply for a job, the name and email you type (and, for a job, the other details and files you choose to send) are sent to the Schritt team by email so we can reply to you, and for profiles to know who has registered.

If you sign in with Google

Schritt receives your name, email address and a Google account id from Google, to identify your account. We also email your name and email to the Schritt team the first time you sign in. Your Google password is never shared with Schritt. Your progress is then stored on our server so it syncs between devices, and a login cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes the cookie and the local copy from this device.

What leaves your device

Your browser loads the app files from the website that hosts it, and that host keeps ordinary server logs. Spoken lessons use your browser's or system's text-to-speech; some voices are online voices run by the browser vendor and may send the spoken text to them. Google's sign-in script loads only after you choose to sign in. There is no analytics, no advertising tracking, no tracking cookies and no third-party fonts.

Health information and HIPAA

Schritt does not ask for, collect or store health information. HIPAA applies to US healthcare providers, health plans and their business associates handling protected health information, so it does not apply to Schritt and we do not claim HIPAA compliance. Please do not enter health information in profile names or custom language packs.

Data we collect, why, and who processes it

Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account id, used only to create and recognise your Schritt account and to sync your progress. Profile registration: the name and email you type, used for the same purpose and to let the Schritt team know a new learner joined. Learning data: lessons completed, streak, gems and settings, used to show your progress. Job applications: the details and files you submit on the Careers page, used only to consider your application. Service providers that process this data for us: Google (sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Upstash (database that stores cloud accounts and progress) and Resend (email delivery). We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Google user data and Limited Use

Schritt's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only the basic profile and email scopes (openid, email, profile). We use them only to sign you in and to run your account. We do not share Google user data with third parties except the service providers listed above that store or deliver it for us, we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it unless you ask us for support, we need to investigate abuse, or the law requires it. You can revoke Schritt's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security of your data

Data is sent over HTTPS. Login cookies are signed, HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite. Cloud data is stored with a managed database provider and access is limited to the Schritt operator. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your own device and browser up to date.

Deleting your data

Email pandyautsav@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the cloud account and its progress within 30 days and confirm by email. Local data on your device is removed with Erase all data on this device in Profile.

Children

Schritt is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live, use it without an account, or with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has signed in, contact pandyautsav@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

Job applications

If you apply through the Careers page, the details and files you submit are emailed to the Schritt team and handled by our email provider on their way. We use them only to consider your application and to reply to you, and we delete them when the hiring process ends unless you ask us to keep them. Please send only what you are comfortable sharing, and do not include government ID numbers or health information.

Your choices

Download my data and Erase all data on this device are in Profile > Privacy, safety and data. To delete a cloud account and its server copy, email pandyautsav@gmail.com from the address on the account. Deleting a local profile removes it from this device immediately.

How long we keep data

On your device until you erase it. Cloud account data until you ask us to delete it or the account has been unused for two years.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app will say so.
